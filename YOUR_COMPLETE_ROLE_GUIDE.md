## 🌍 WHERE DO ADVERSE EVENTS COME FROM?

### **Real-World Sources:**

```
HOSPITALS
├─ Patient has reaction → Doctor reports
├─ Medical team documents → Pharmacy reports
└─ Result: Official hospital adverse event report

PHARMACIES
├─ Patient picks up medicine
├─ Patient calls: "I'm having bad reaction"
└─ Result: Pharmacy sends report to company

DOCTORS' OFFICES
├─ Patient calls: "Medicine made me sick"
├─ Doctor documents → Sends to company
└─ Result: Direct doctor report

PATIENTS (Direct)
├─ Patient calls 1-800 number on medicine box
├─ "I had allergic reaction"
└─ Result: Direct patient report

PATIENT FAMILIES
├─ Family member calls company
├─ "My relative had severe reaction"
└─ Result: Family member report

MEDICAL JOURNALS
├─ Article published about side effects
├─ Company researches → Creates case
└─ Result: Literature-based case

REGULATORS (FDA, EMA, CDSCO)
├─ Regulator sends complaint to company
├─ "Report this case immediately"
└─ Result: Urgent regulatory case
```
