## 🌍 WHERE DO ADVERSE EVENTS COME FROM?

### **Real Sources of Adverse Events:**

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    REAL WORLD SOURCES                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. HOSPITALS
   ├─ Patient admitted with adverse reaction
   ├─ Doctor documents in medical records
   ├─ Hospital pharmacist identifies
   └─ Hospital reports to pharmaceutical company

2. PHARMACIES
   ├─ Patient picks up medicine
   ├─ Patient calls back: "I have rash now"
   ├─ Pharmacist documents
   └─ Pharmacy reports to company

3. DOCTORS' OFFICES
   ├─ Patient calls: "Medicine made me sick"
   ├─ Doctor documents in chart
   ├─ Doctor's staff reports to company
   └─ Company receives report

4. PATIENTS DIRECTLY
   ├─ Patient calls 1-800 number on package
   ├─ Patient reports: "I had bad reaction"
   ├─ Company takes the report
   └─ Report entered into system

5. PATIENT FAMILIES
   ├─ Patient had serious reaction at home
   ├─ Family member calls company
   ├─ Reports to pharmacovigilance department
   └─ Case created in system

6. MEDICAL LITERATURE
   ├─ Journal article published
   ├─ Mentions adverse event with your drug
   ├─ Company researches it
   └─ Case created from published data

7. HEALTH REGULATORS
   ├─ FDA receives complaint from patient
   ├─ FDA sends to company: "Report this"
   ├─ Company must respond
   └─ Case created with urgency flag
```
