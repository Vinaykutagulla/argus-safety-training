## WEEK 5: FINAL EXAM
### EXERCISE 5.2 - Comprehensive Case Assessment

**Date: ________     Student: __________**

**SCENARIO 1 - Read this carefully:**

```
"A 68-year-old male patient (RK) in Delhi, India started taking
a new diabetes medication (Metformin 500mg) on January 10.
On January 14 (Day 5), he experienced sudden severe chest pain,
shortness of breath, and collapsed. He was taken to hospital
where he died two hours later. The case was reported by his
physician on January 15 at 10 AM. Family suspects the medication
caused the heart attack. Patient had history of hypertension
but no previous heart disease."
```

**ANSWER ALL QUESTIONS (No peeking at notes!):**

1. **Seriousness Assessment:**
   ```
   Is this serious? YES / NO
   
   Reasons:
   □ Death
   □ Life-threatening
   □ Hospitalization
   □ Permanent disability
   □ Other: ________________
   
   Justify your answer: ________________________
   ```

2. **Regulatory Requirement:**
   ```
   What reporting rule applies?
   □ 7-day (unlisted serious)
   □ 15-day (serious unexpected)
   □ Quarterly (non-serious)
   
   Why: ________________________
   
   Due Date: ________________
   
   Days from receipt to due date: _______ days
   ```

3. **MedDRA Coding:**
   ```
   Patient's symptoms: "Chest pain, shortness of breath"
   
   MedDRA Term 1: ____________________________
   Code: ____________________________
   
   MedDRA Term 2: ____________________________
   Code: ____________________________
   ```

4. **Causality Assessment:**
   ```
   How likely is medication causing death?
   □ Probable (strong relationship)
   □ Possible (could be other factors)
   □ Unlikely (other clear cause)
   □ Unrelated (no temporal relationship)
   
   Justify: ________________________
   ________________________
   ```

5. **Immediate Actions:**
   ```
   What would you do RIGHT NOW?
   Action 1: ________________________
   Action 2: ________________________
   Action 3: ________________________
   
   Who would you tell: ________________________
   ```
