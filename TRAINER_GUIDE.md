## 📅 WEEKLY STRUCTURE (What to Do Each Day)

### WEEK 1: FOUNDATION (Understanding Pharmacovigilance)

#### **DAY 1: System Orientation (90 minutes)**
**Your Job:**
1. **Morning (8:00-8:30 AM):** 
   - Welcome student formally
   - Explain: "We're not reading about PV, we're DOING it"
   - Show 5-minute system overview

2. **Student Work (8:30-9:00 AM):**
   - Have them read TRAINING_GUIDE.md "Pharmacovigilance Basics" section
   - Watch you as you navigate system once
   - Take notes on key concepts

3. **Hands-On (9:00-10:00 AM):**
   - Student navigates themselves while you watch
   - They complete EXERCISE 1.1 in STUDENT_WORKBOOK.md
   - You help when they get lost

4. **Review (10:00-10:30 AM):**
   - Check their worksheet
   - Ask: "What's in the Dashboard?" "Where do you add a case?"
   - Correct any misunderstandings

**Success Metric:** Student can navigate to 5 main sections without help

---

#### **DAY 2: Seriousness Matters (90 minutes)**
**Your Job:**
1. **Morning (8:00-8:30 AM):**
   - Review yesterday's learning
   - NEW CONCEPT: "Seriousness = Urgency"
   - Real example: "If a case says DEATH, I drop everything"

2. **Student Work (8:30-9:00 AM):**
   - Read: QUICK_REFERENCE_CARD.md "Seriousness Decision Matrix"
   - You explain: Why some cases are serious, why others aren't

3. **Hands-On (9:00-10:00 AM):**
   - Student opens case ARG-001 in system
   - They fill out EXERCISE 1.2 in workbook
   - They identify why ARG-001 is serious

4. **Review (10:00-10:30 AM):**
   - Check their answers
   - Quiz them: "Is a mild headache serious? Why not?"
   - Real scenario: "You get a call - patient fainted after medicine. Serious?"

**Success Metric:** Student can correctly identify serious vs non-serious

---

#### **DAY 3: Regulatory Deadlines (90 minutes)**
**Your Job:**
1. **Morning:**
   - CONCEPT: "Weekends COUNT. Holidays COUNT."
   - Show calendar: Count the actual days
   - This is their first "Oh wow!" moment

2. **Student Work (8:30-9:00 AM):**
   - Read: TRAINING_GUIDE.md "ICH E2A Requirements" section
   - Understand: 7-day rule, 15-day rule, Quarterly rule
   - You explain when each applies

3. **Hands-On (9:00-10:00 AM):**
   - Student opens Reports → Expedited in system
   - See real deadlines with countdown clocks
   - Complete EXERCISE 1.3 "Deadline Drill" in workbook
   - Calculate 7 different deadline scenarios

4. **Review:**
   - Check calculations
   - Emphasize: "Wrong deadline = regulatory violation = your fault"

**Success Metric:** All deadline calculations 100% correct

---

#### **DAY 4: Workflow Stages (90 minutes)**
**Your Job:**
1. **Morning:**
   - Show them the 6 stages visually
   - Explain: "Cases FLOW through these stages"
   - INTAKE → TRIAGE → DATA ENTRY → MEDICAL → QC → LOCKED

2. **Student Work:**
   - Read: TRAINING_GUIDE.md "6-Stage Workflow" section
   - You explain each stage with real example

3. **Hands-On:**
   - Student opens Workflow section in system
   - See pipeline with case counts
   - Complete EXERCISE 1.4 in workbook
   - Document what happens at each stage

4. **Review:**
   - Ask: "Where would a case go if it needs medical review?"
   - Role-play: "You're the Data Entry person. What do you do?"

**Success Metric:** Student can explain all 6 stages

---

#### **DAY 5: WEEK 1 ASSESSMENT (90 minutes)**
**Your Job:**
1. **Morning (8:00-8:30 AM):**
   - Review all 5 learning points
   - Student checks own notes

2. **Assessment (8:30-10:00 AM):**
   - NO HELP. This is their test.
   - Give them the scenario in STUDENT_WORKBOOK.md "Week 1 Assessment"
   - They must answer WITHOUT looking at materials
   - You fill out scoring rubric

3. **Review & Feedback (10:00-10:30 AM):**
   - Go over answers together
   - Identify what they understood vs what they struggled with
   - Plan for Week 2

**Passing Score:** 4/5 questions correct

**If they fail:**
- Don't let them continue until they understand
- Spend extra time on weak areas
- Retest before moving to Week 2

## 🎓 TIPS FOR BEING A GREAT TRAINER

### ✅ DO:
- **Be available** - Students will be stuck sometimes
- **Ask questions** - Let them figure it out, guide with questions
- **Show real consequences** - "This deadline miss = FDA warning letter"
- **Be encouraging** - Learning a new system is hard
- **Document everything** - Keep records of their progress
- **Give specific feedback** - "Good MedDRA selection because..."
- **Make it real** - Connect to actual pharmaceutical safety
- **Celebrate progress** - Week 1 → Week 5 is a JOURNEY

### ❌ DON'T:
- **Do the work for them** - They'll never learn
- **Rush them** - Accuracy > Speed (initially)
- **Skip the theory** - They need to understand WHY
- **Be harsh** - Mistakes are learning opportunities
- **Assume they know** - Ask before you explain
- **Give blank access** - Guide them through system
- **Let them skip** - If they don't pass, reteach and retest

## 🚨 PROBLEM SCENARIOS & HOW TO HANDLE THEM

### Student is Moving Too Slowly
**Problem:** Taking 30+ minutes per case
**Solution:** 
1. Don't rush them (accuracy first)
2. Identify bottleneck: What's slowing them down?
3. Extra practice on that skill
4. Time will improve naturally

### Student is Guessing at Seriousness
**Problem:** "I'm not sure if it's serious" with no clear reasoning
**Solution:**
1. Make them read seriousness definition again
2. Have them checklist against criteria
3. Ask: "Does patient need hospital?" If YES → serious
4. Practice with 5 more scenarios until they get it

### Student Forgets MedDRA Coding
**Problem:** Uses patient's words instead of medical terms
**Solution:**
1. Stop them immediately
2. Show them the rule: "NEVER patient's words"
3. Have them search MedDRA together
4. Make them redo the case correctly
5. Practice drills until it's automatic

### Student Fails Assessment
**Problem:** Scored below 80% on Week assessment
**Solution:**
1. Identify specific areas of failure
2. Reteach those concepts
3. Additional practice
4. Retest after extra training
5. Must pass before advancing to next week

### Student Struggles with Deadlines
**Problem:** Consistently miscalculating deadlines
**Solution:**
1. Calendar exercise: Count on calendar together
2. Point out: "Weekends COUNT"
3. Create deadline worksheet they can use
4. Drill 10 deadline scenarios
5. Time pressure practice
